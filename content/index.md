hello, there! my name is Ashutosh (Ashu), and I am a PhD candidate at Purdue University in the field of [chemical engineering](https://www2.aiche.org/k-12/what-do-chemical-engineers-do). 

hailing from [Rajasthan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajasthan), India, I have been fortunate enough to travel across the globe and experience a myriad range of cultures. I began my schooling in Kota, moved to Pune, followed by a short stint in Mumbai before finishing high school in Istanbul, Turkey. I carry an inquisitive attitude and take pleasure in small and beautiful things that life offers. When not busy suffering through grad school, I enjoy spending time with my friends, playing badminton, and catching up with science fiction movies/shows. 

thank you for taking the time to visit this website, and stay tuned for regular (or maybe not-so-regular updates) in the near future...cheers!
