Problem 4.2

Q's

Do electrochemical potentials equal each other over the salt bridge? (Flow of ions is permitted?)

Each cell is labelled by phase, α∣β∥β′∣α′, with

  • Ucell​=Vα′−Vα
  • Reversible potential @ equilibrium: μeα​−μeα′​=FU
  • Species in adjacent phases are at equilibrium.

(a) Cu(s)​∣0.001MCuSO4(aq)​∥0.1MCuSO4(aq)​∣Cu(s)​

LHS: RHS: ​Cu⇌Cu2++2e−⇒μCuα​=μCu2+β​+2μeα​⇒μeα​=21​(μCuα​−μCu2+β​)Cu⇌Cu2++2e−⇒μCuα′​=μCu2+β′​+2μeα′​⇒μeα′​=21​(μCuα′​−μCu2+β′​)​ ∴Ucell​=2F1​(μCuα​−μCu2+β​−μCuα′​+μCu2+β′​)​

(b) Ag(s)​∣AgCl(s)​∣HCl(aq)​∣H2(g)​,Pt(s)​ — with μeα​=μeβ​:

LHS: RHS: ​AgCl+e−⇌Ag+Cl−⇒μAgClβ​+μeα​=μAgα​+μCl−β′​⇒μeα​=μAgα​−μCl−β′​−μAgClβ​H2​⇌2H++2e−⇒μH2​α′​=2μH+β′​+2μeα′​⇒μeα′​=21​(μH2​α​−2μH+β′​)​ ∴Ucell​=F1​(μAgα​−μCl−β′​−μAgClβ​−21​(μH2​α​−2μH+β′​))​

(c) Zn(s)​∣ZnO(s)​∣KOH(aq)​∣O2(g)​,Pt(s)​ — with μeα​=μeβ​:

LHS: RHS: ​Zn+2OH−⇌ZnO+H2​O+2e−⇒μZnα​+2μOH−β′​=μZnOβ​+μH2​Oβ′​+2μeα​⇒μeα​=21​(μZnα​+2μOH−β′​−μZnOβ​−μH2​Oβ′​)4OH−⇌O2​+4e−+2H2​O⇒4μOH−β′​=μO2​α′​+4μeα′​+2μH2​Oβ′​⇒μeα′​=41​(4μOH−β′​−μO2​α′​−2μH2​Oβ′​)​ ∴Ucell​=2F1​(μZnα​+2μOH−β′​−μZnOβ​−μH2​Oβ′​−21​(4μOH−β′​−μO2​α′​−2μH2​Oβ′​))=2F1​(μZnα​−μZnOβ​+21​μO2​α′​)​

(d) Pt(s)​,O2(g)​∣NaOH(aq)​∥NH3(aq)​,NaNO2(aq)​∣Pt(s)​ — with μH2​Oβ​=μH2​Oβ′​ (μH2​O​=?):

LHS: RHS: ​4OH−⇌O2​+2H2​O+4e−⇒4μOH−β​=μO2​α​+2μH2​Oβ​+4μeα​⇒μeα​=41​(4μOH−β​−μO2​α​−2μH2​Oβ​)NO2−​+6e−+5H2​O⇌NH3​+7OH−⇒μNO2−​β′​+6μeα′​+5μH2​Oβ′​=μNH3​β′​+7μOH−β​⇒μeα′​=61​(μNH3​β′​+7μOH−β​−5μH2​Oβ′​−μNO2−​β′​)​ ∴Ucell​=2F1​(21​(4μOH−β​−μO2​α​−2μH2​Oβ​)−31​(μNH3​β′​+7μOH−β​−5μH2​Oβ′​−μNO2−​β′​))​

Nernst correction for the OER, 2H2​O⇌O2​+4H++4e− (2H2​O−O2​−4H+=4e−):

(UO2​/H2​O​−UH2​/H+θ​)=(UO2​/H2​Oθ​−UH2​/H+θ​)−4FRT​ln(pO2​​cH+4​1​)=1.23+FRT​lncH+​=1.23−0.0591(pH) V vs. SHE

Ni⇌Ni(aq)2+​+2e− — Nernst correction for the Ni electrode:

(UNi/Ni2+​−UH2​/H+​)=(UNi/Ni2+θ​−UH2​/H+​)+2FRT​ln(cNi2+​\cancelto1cNi​​)⇒UNi/Ni2+​=−0.257+FRT​ln(cNi2+​)1/2←not dependent on pH

(sketch: V vs. SHE against pH from 0 to 14 — the OER line from 1.23 V, the HER line from 0 V falling to −0.8274 V at pH 14, and the horizontal Ni/Ni²⁺ line at −0.257 V crossing the HER line at pH 4.3; the region below pH 4.3 between them is shaded as “Ni corrosion”.)

Stability:

  • (UNi/Ni2+​−UH2​/H+​)<0 — non-spontaneous @ pH < 4.3 (Ni more negative → will be oxidized)
  • (UNi/Ni​−UH2​/H+​)>0 — spontaneous @ pH > 4.3 (H₂ more negative → will be oxidized)

∴ Ni will be oxidized/corrode below pH 4.3 in acidic solution.
