Notes on density functional theory (DFT 697)

Notes on density functional theory (DFT 697)

Oct 26, 20266 min read

Handwritten class notes from DFT 697 (Spring 2023), transcribed into Markdown and LaTeX. Each lecture page has the typed version first, with the original handwritten pages in a collapsible box underneath.

  • Original handwritten notes (PDF, 43 MB)

Lectures

  • Lecture 03 · 2023-01-17 — Many particles in a box, fermion antisymmetry and Slater determinants, the Hohenberg–Kohn theorems, functionals, and the 1D kinetic-energy functional.
  • Lecture 04 · 2023-01-19 — Using the simple kinetic-energy functional with Lagrange multipliers — particle in a box, a delta-function spike, a linear potential, the 1D Thomas–Fermi atom — and adding Coulomb and exchange terms.
  • Lecture 05 · 2023-01-24 — A one-dimensional atom with delta-function interactions, DFT and the TISE with periodic boundary conditions, the Dirac comb, Bloch’s theorem and the origin of band structure.
  • Lecture 06 · 2023-01-26 — Solving the periodic TISE with k-points, then Kohn–Sham DFT — the fictitious non-interacting system, the Kohn–Sham equations, the self-consistent algorithm, and a worked two-fermion example.
  • Lecture 07 (Thermo) · 2023-01-31 — Basis sets and energy cutoffs, the hierarchy of exchange–correlation functionals (LDA, GGA, meta-GGA, hybrids), major limitations of DFT (band gaps, self-interaction, van der Waals), and an introduction to surface thermodynamics and the grand potential.
  • Lecture 08 (Thermo) · 2023-02-07 — Interfacial thermodynamics with the grand potential — excess quantities, surface tension and the Gibbs–Duhem relation for interfaces — and how to approximate the internal energy, entropy and chemical potentials (equipartition and statistical mechanics) to build surface phase diagrams.
  • Lecture 09 (Thermo) · 2023-02-09 — Ideal-gas partition functions for internal energy and entropy, real gases via fugacity, bulk stability limits for H on Ni(111) and CO oxidation on RuO₂(110), zeolite “surface” energies, and surface energies of metals in vacuum.
  • Lecture 10 (Wulff & Langmuir) · 2023-02-14 — Nanoparticle shapes from surface free energies — the Wulff theorem and its “proof”, generalizing Wulff to supports (adhesion energy) and adsorbates — and deriving the Langmuir isotherm from the grand canonical partition function.
  • Lecture 11 (Langmuir) · 2023-02-16 — Langmuir isotherm behaviour, then adsorbate–adsorbate interactions with the 1D Ising model solved by a transfer matrix, the weak-interaction limit, and how attractive or repulsive interactions reshape isotherms.
  • Lecture 12 (Kinetics) · 2023-02-23 — Kinetics with DFT — transition state theory for surface reactions, activities of gas, liquid and surface species, chemical potentials of adsorbates from partition functions, and the harmonic TST rate expression, including reactions with gas-phase species.
  • Lecture 13 · 2023-02-28 — TST for gas–solid (Eley–Rideal) reactions, TST without statistical mechanics, collision theory for adsorption (and its equivalence to TST), thermodynamic consistency of rate constants, and an introduction to microkinetic modelling with quasi-equilibrium and rate-limiting steps.
  • Lecture 14 · 2023-03-02 — Microkinetic analysis of ammonia synthesis — the full steady-state solution and limiting cases where N₂ dissociation or NH hydrogenation is rate-limiting, with the approach-to-equilibrium factor.
  • Lecture 15 · 2023-03-07 — Volcano plots for heterogeneous catalysis — deriving the rate vs. dissociative adsorption energy with a BEP relation, how the volcano maximum shifts with approach to equilibrium, pressure and temperature, and a second example with a molecular precursor state.
  • Lecture 16 · 2023-03-21 — Volcanoes with more than one rate-controlling step — the full steady-state solution, the overall energy diagram and parameter choices, and how the legs of a volcano correspond to different rate-limiting steps, with Sabatier upper bounds.
  • Lecture 17 · 2023-03-30 — Term-project guidance (e.g. HER on Pt(111)), coverage-dependent binding energies and their effect on volcano plots, and setting up an ammonia-synthesis volcano with entropies, BEP relations and scaling relations between intermediates.
  • Lecture 18 · 2023-04-04 — Ammonia synthesis as a model reaction — expressing all step energies through scaling relations in one descriptor, combining BEP and TST, the resulting two-legged volcano, and the bond-order-conservation basis of scaling relationships (including multidentate species).
  • Lecture 19 · 2023-04-11 — Group-additivity style scaling relations across many adsorbates on one surface, and the classic scaling/BEP analysis of ethanol decomposition on transition metals — coverages from thermodynamics, CO* interactions, BEP barriers for C–C and C–O scission, and step sites.
  • Lecture 20 (CHE) · 2023-04-18 — Electrochemistry with DFT — electrified interfaces, chemical vs electrochemical steps, the computational hydrogen electrode (CHE) for μ(H⁺)+μ(e⁻), and free-energy diagrams, onset potentials and scaling relations for the oxygen reduction reaction (ORR).
  • Lecture 21 (D-band) · 2023-04-20 — Electrocatalysis continued — limiting-legs and Sabatier analysis of the ORR with voltage-dependent rate constants and activity volcanoes vs ΔE_O — followed by an introduction to d-band analysis (density of states, tight binding, Newns–Anderson model).
  • Lecture 22 (Zeolites) · 2023-04-25 — Guest lecture on zeolites — k-point density, levels of solvent modelling in microporous materials, water in Sn-Beta zeolites, AIMD-based entropies (solid-like + gas-like), and TST/DFT kinetic modelling with low-frequency modes.
  • Lecture 23 · 2023-04-27 — Final lecture — degree of rate control in a zeolite microkinetic model, then a review of chemical potentials for ideal gases and surface species, the failure of the harmonic approximation for low-frequency modes, and particle-in-a-box and 2D ideal-gas treatments of adsorbates.

Homework

  • HWs · 2023-01-30 — Section divider page for the DFT 697 homework sets (no content on this page).
  • HW 01 · 2023-01-27 — Homework 1 solutions — two fermions in a box via a Slater determinant and their expectation energy, the 1D δ-function well with simple DFT (diverges), the DFT harmonic oscillator vs the exact result, and a spherical Thomas–Fermi-like atom.
  • HW 02 · 2023-02-15 — Homework 2 solutions — band energies of a periodic Dirac comb from the Bloch condition (discrete sums vs integrals for P = 10 and 20, one to three bands) compared with DFT, and simple DFT for a sinusoidal potential.
  • HW 03 · 2023-03-01 — Homework 3 solutions — an ab-initio phase diagram for H adsorption on Pt(111) (surface and subsurface sites), T/P relations for μ_H and a coverage heat map, and the Wulff shape of a Pt nanoparticle with support and adsorbate effects.
  • HW 04 · 2023-04-04 — Homework 4 solutions — showing that the fully steady-state A ⇌ A* ⇌ B* ⇌ B mechanism reduces to quasi-equilibrium of steps 1 and 3, both by direct limits and by a perturbation expansion in the slow rate constants.
