Problem 3.39 — truncated cone, worked in class

(sketch: the truncated cone with uniform flow v​=−Ue^z​ coming down from the top; radii R₁ (top) and R₂ (bottom), height H, truncated a distance l from the tip at the origin, half-angle θ = α.)

Questions put to the class:

  • How many surfaces?
  • Draw them out & find n^.
  • What is the mass flow rate formula?
∬n^⋅v​dA(dxdy)→dA in the original coordinates

e^θ​→xyz? → dot w/ v​ → how? Some unit basis transformation (θ → xyz), from geometry:

e^θ​=cosθcosϕe^x​+cosθsinϕe^y​−sinθe^z​

Mass flow rate ⇒ ρ∬(n^⋅v​)dA (“volume” flow times density).

Surfaces: (1) top face, n^=e^z​; (2) cone side, n^=e^θ​; (3) bottom face, n^=−e^z​.

On the side (θ = α):

​cosθcosϕcosθsinϕ−sinθ​​⋅​00−U​​=Usinθ→∫02π​∫l2+R22​​(l+H)2+R12​​​ρUsinαdA;dA=rsinθdϕdr(θ=α)

with 0<ϕ<2π, and the limits on r from the slant lengths of the two right triangles (l, R₂) and (l + H, R₁).

Transcriber’s note: the dot product as written gives +Usinθ; with the z entries (−sinθ)(−U) this is correct. The r limits are as written; with the tip at the origin, the slant lengths are l2+R22​​ and (l+H)2+R12​​ only if R₂ and R₁ are the radii at those heights.
