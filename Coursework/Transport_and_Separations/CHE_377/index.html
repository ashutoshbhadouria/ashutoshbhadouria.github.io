CHE 377: Momentum transfer (TA notes)

CHE 377: Momentum transfer (TA notes)

Oct 26, 20269 min read

Notes I wrote as a teaching assistant for CHE 377 (Fall 2023), exported from OneNote and transcribed into Markdown and LaTeX. They are split into recitations, office-hour (OH) notes and exam reviews. Each note shows the typed version first, with the original pages in a collapsible box at the top and a link to open just those pages as a PDF.

  • Recitations (PDF) · Office hours (PDF) · Reviews (PDF)

Lectures

  • Lecture 09-26 · 2023-09-25 — Recitation 9/26 as annotated in class — review of the stress tensor, viscosity and static-fluid problems (shear and normal stresses on a fluid element, incompressibility, Π split into normal and shear parts), the manometer review, and a worked solution of the two-fluid manometer pressure difference.

Other notes

  • Derivation · 2023-09-21 — Office-hours derivation of film flow down an inclined plane — the force balance on a fluid element with the stress tensor, the x-component momentum balance, the Newtonian constitutive equation, and the velocity profile v_z(x) = (ρg cos β/μ)(Hx − x²/2).
  • Problem Clarification · 2023-10-27 — Office-hours clarification on computing the force on a surface from the total stress tensor — integration bounds over the surface, evaluating at the surface coordinates, and the wire-coating example giving F = (2πμVL/ln κ) ê_z because the pressure term integrates to zero around the wire.
  • Drag coefficient · 2023-08-28 — Recitation notes (weeks 2–3) — characteristic areas for drag coefficients, then the Reynolds transport theorem worksheet (material vs. control volume, D/Dt, momentum flux through a surface) with worked problems on momentum flow through a hemisphere, average and maximum velocity in a slit, and gravity components on an incline.
  • Derivation (2) · 2023-09-29 — Office-hours derivation of the Newtonian stress tensor in cylindrical coordinates — ∇v as a dyadic product with the del operator, derivatives of the unit vectors, the transpose (∇v)ᵀ, and the resulting symmetric τ components.
  • FL Lab2 · 2023-09-29 — Office-hours overview of Fundamental Lab 2, turbulent flow in a pipe — Reynolds number, Darcy friction factor (Blasius and Colebrook correlations, Fanning f = f_D/4), the Darcy–Weisbach equation, the experimental setup with a differential pressure gauge and rotameter, and pre-lab hints for a force balance relating wall shear stress to Δp.
  • Week 03 · 2023-09-04 — Office-hours sketch of spherical coordinates — the polar angle θ from z and azimuth φ in the xy plane, with the full-sphere limits 0 < φ < 2π, 0 < θ < π.
  • FL4 Lab Guide · 2023-11-07 — Lab guide for Fundamental Lab 4, centrifugal pump characteristic curves — theory from the energy balance to the MEB, pump efficiency and brake horsepower, discharge and suction heads, the lab procedure, net head with piping and fitting friction, the dimensionless pump coefficients C_H, C_Q, C_P and η, and what to plot, discuss and include in the error analysis.
  • Week 04 · 2023-09-11 — Section divider page for the week 4 recitations (no content on this page).
  • 6.39 · 2023-10-29 — Office-hours help on Problem 6.39 — the pressure field in inclined flow, P = −ρg_z z + (ΔP/L)x + P₀, from integrating ∂P/∂z and matching ∂P/∂x, and integrating radial continuity to get v_r = c₁/r.
  • L6 Surface Tension, Reynolds Transport Theorem · 2023-09-04 — Office-hours worked example for lecture 6 (surface tension and the Reynolds transport theorem) — the momentum RTT for steady flow through a 90° bend with uniform inlet and outlet velocities, giving ΣF = −ρv₁²A₁ê_i − ρv₂²A₂ê_k.
  • 7.10 · 2023-11-06 — Office-hours help on Problem 7.10, radial flow between parallel plates — continuity gives v_r = f(z)/r, the slow (developed) flow assumption drops the inertial term, and separation of variables r dP/dr = μ f″ = λ splits the problem.
  • Week of 7th Nov · 2023-11-07 — Recitation for the week of 7 November — centrifugal pump characteristic curves from the energy balance on a control volume (macro energy balance, mechanical energy balance, pump efficiency, brake horsepower, discharge and suction head), the pump lab, and boundary-layer drag on a flat plate.
  • Week of 7th Nov (2) · 2023-11-07 — In-class copy of the week-of-7-November recitation — pump-curve annotations (head loss from Δp/ρg, Δz and Δ⟨v⟩² across suction and discharge), inviscid potential flow around a sphere (τ = 0, Π = −pI), interface stress conditions, and the flat-plate drag force F = τ_w·A with a units check.
  • Week of 7th Nov (3) · 2023-11-07 — Second in-class copy of the week-of-7-November recitation — the pump energy balance traced as a PFD, a head-loss example lifting water 30 m from a river, the inviscid-flow argument via the Re → ∞ limit and dynamic pressure, and the flat-plate drag geometry with the boundary-layer edge at v = 0.99 v∞.
  • Recitation 09 12 · 2023-09-11 — Recitation 9/12 as annotated in class — the Stokes’-law viscosity lab and pre-lab force balance, plus a worked setup of the truncated-cone control volume (counting surfaces, normals, the ê_θ transformation and the mass-flow integral over the cone side).
  • Recitation 09 12 (master) · 2023-09-11 — Recitation 9/12 worksheet (master) — Fundamental Lab 1 on fluid viscosity by Stokes’ law (drag on a falling sphere, lab logistics and report structure, pre-lab derivation of μ from a terminal-velocity force balance), then problems on a truncated-cone control volume, an average velocity in cylindrical coordinates and gravity in wire coating.
  • Recitation 09 12 (master) (2) · 2023-09-11 — A second copy of the 9/12 recitation master (Stokes’-law viscosity lab and pre-lab, truncated-cone, average-velocity and wire-coating problems), with extra sketches of the spherical unit vectors around the cone’s control surfaces.
  • Week of 23rd · 2023-10-23 — Recitation for the week of 23 October — macroscopic control-volume vs. microscopic balances (continuity and Cauchy/Navier–Stokes), axial annular drag in wire coating with and without a pressure gradient (velocity profile, flow rate by integration by parts, force on the wire), and upward pressure-driven pipe flow of a power-law fluid.
  • Week of 23rd (2) · 2023-10-23 — In-class copy of the week-of-23-October recitation — simplifying the cylindrical Navier–Stokes equations term by term for wire-coating flow, the pressure gradient Δp/L for Problem 6.49, solving for v_z = c₁ ln r + c₂ with the wire and shell boundary conditions, and setting up the force on the wire.
  • Week of 23rd (3) · 2023-10-23 — Second in-class copy of the week-of-23-October recitation — a four-step recipe for flow problems (characterize the flow, continuity, Navier–Stokes, BCs), the wire-coating solution with ∂P/∂z = ΔP/L, and the setup for Problem 6.49 with a pressure gradient.
  • Week 25th Sept · 2023-09-25 — Recitation worksheet for the week of 25 September — viscosity as shear stress over rate of shear strain, the Newtonian constitutive equations for shear and normal stresses, isotropic vs. anisotropic stress and the total stress tensor Π = τ − pI, a manometer review (Pascal’s principle, hydrostatic pressure), and problems on a two-fluid manometer and an incline-film coordinate system.
  • Week 25th Sept (2) · 2023-09-25 — A clean second copy of the week-of-25-September recitation worksheet (viscosity, Newtonian stresses, the stress tensor, manometer review, and the manometer and incline-film problems).
  • Week of 27th Nov · 2023-11-14 — Recitation for the week of 27 November — the shift from flow profiles to large-scale quantities, macroscopic mass, momentum and energy balances (stream sums with β and α), and problems on a Venturi meter, the maximum siphon height before the liquid boils, and tank-and-hose flow with friction.
  • Week of 27th Nov (2) · 2023-11-14 — In-class copy of the week-of-27-November recitation — worked Venturi-meter pressure drop from the mass balance and MEB (−Δp = ρ⟨v₂⟩²/2 · [1 − (D₂/D₁)⁴]), the siphon exit velocity v₂ = √(2g(z₁ − z₂)), and the maximum siphon height where the pressure reaches the vapor pressure.
  • Week of 27th Nov (3) · 2023-11-14 — A second in-class copy of the 27 November recitation, then the final recitation (week of 5 December) — velocities in a converging 20° bend, combined pressure-driven and drag (Couette–Poiseuille) flow in a slit solved from Navier–Stokes with the location of maximum velocity, and the macroscopic momentum balance for the force to support a 90° expanding pipe bend.
  • Week of 31st · 2023-10-30 — Recitation for the week of 30 October — why turbulent flow needs empirical correlations, the fluid drag force on a pipe wall, laminar pipe flow (pressure and velocity profiles, Q, Π and F_drag = πR²(P₀ − P_L)), and turbulent flow in smooth, branched and rough pipes with the Prandtl and Colebrook correlations and iterative algorithms.
  • Week of 31st (2) · 2023-10-30 — In-class copy of the week-of-30-October recitation — turbulent eddies, the drag force on the pipe wall, and annotations on the friction-factor algorithms, including an iterative fixed-point solver for the Prandtl correlation and a worked numeric setup (P₀ = 65 psi, P_L = 0).
  • Week of 31st (3) · 2023-10-30 — Second in-class copy of the week-of-30-October recitation — turbulence as a 3D PDE needing CFD or Darcy friction correlations, a series-pipe example (1-in and ½-in pipes from 60 psig to 0 psig) worked with Algorithm 1, and notes on the branched-pipe flow split.
  • CHE_377_Reviews — Exam review sessions for CHE 377 — MT1 (Reynolds transport theorem, mass and momentum balances, force on a pipe bend), an OH review of two-layer incline flow, the exam 2 algorithm with falling-film and power-law pipe-flow problems, MT3 boundary layers with sailboat and roof-wind problems, and the final review (pipe-bend force by RTT vs. macroscopic balance, radial flow between disks).
  • Notes 2023-10-23 · 2023-10-23 — Office-hours notes (23 October) — resolving gravity on an incline, falling-film flow outside a vertical rod (velocity profile with a zero-shear free surface), and setting up flow down an inclined plane with continuity, Navier–Stokes and boundary conditions.
  • Notes 2023-11-28 · 2023-11-28 — Office-hours worked problems (28 November) — turbulent drag on both sides of a flat plate from C_D = 0.031/Re^(1/7), and a pipe-bend problem using the mass balance, the MEB for the inlet pressure, the outlet direction at 40°, and the momentum balance.
